Where the money comes from

Nobody is predicting anything.

This isn't a bet on a stock going up. It's a rounding rule, written into how brokers settle fractional shares. It pays the same whether the market is green or red that week.

01 / YOU SPEND 25¢

One share. Every account.

A company trading under a dollar announces a 1-for-20 reverse split to keep its listing. You buy a single share in each brokerage you hold — pocket change apiece. RSAMAXXED places all ten orders in parallel and journals every fill.

02 / IT SHRINKS

Your share becomes a sliver.

On the effective date, twenty old shares collapse into one new share. You held one. Now you hold five hundredths of one. On paper, you own a fraction of a company.

03 / YOU KEEP $4.75

The broker makes you whole.

Rather than settle a fraction, some brokers round you up to a full share — now marked near twenty times what you paid. You sell it. The difference is money that did not exist ten seconds ago.

Not every broker rounds up, and not every split qualifies. That is precisely why you run it across ten of them at once — and why RSAMAXXED only counts a sale that actually cleared. See the full mechanism →
